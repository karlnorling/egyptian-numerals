Egyptian Numerals

Draw any number the way it was carved in ancient Egypt: strokes, hobbles, coils of rope, lotuses, fingers, tadpoles and the god Heh, with fractions as a scribe wrote them. SVG with zero dependencies, plus PNG, JPEG and WebP.

Try any number

The playground draws whatever you type, breaks it into base-10 digits, and lets you download SVG, PNG, JPEG or WebP.

Egyptian fractions

Fractions become unit fractions under the mouth sign, and 2/3, as scribes wrote them: 0.75 is 1/2 + 1/4. See how.

Zero dependencies

@egyptian-numerals/core turns a number into an SVG string in any JavaScript runtime.

Images and CLI

@egyptian-numerals/raster renders PNG, JPEG and WebP with sharp, and @egyptian-numerals/cli does it from the terminal.
