# Egyptian Numerals

Stories for [`@egyptian-numerals/core`](https://www.npmjs.com/package/@egyptian-numerals/core), which renders any number as [Egyptian hieroglyphic numerals](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Egyptian_numerals) in SVG.

- **Number Builder**: type any number in the `value` control and adjust every `toSvg` option.
- **Digits**: every digit from 0 to 9.

Each power of ten has a sign, repeated as often as its digit: stroke, hobble, coil of rope, lotus, finger, tadpole and Heh. Fractions become unit fractions under the mouth sign, or 2/3. Zero is nfr, and numbers stop at 9,999,999.

For downloads and shareable links, use the [playground](../playground/) in the docs.
